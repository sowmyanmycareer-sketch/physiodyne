What We Offer

Our Specialisations

Comprehensive physiotherapy services designed to get you moving better, faster.

🦴

Spine Rehabilitation

Expert treatment for back pain, disc herniation, sciatica, and postural issues using advanced manual therapy and exercises.

⚽

Sports Injury Recovery

Rapid return-to-sport programmes for ligament tears, muscle strains, tendinopathies, and overuse injuries.

🤲

Manual Therapy

Hands-on mobilisation and manipulation techniques to restore joint mobility and reduce pain effectively.

💪

Strength & Conditioning

Targeted exercise programmes to rebuild muscle strength, improve endurance, and prevent re-injury.

⚡

Electrotherapy

TENS, ultrasound, and IFT modalities to accelerate tissue healing and manage acute and chronic pain.

🧘

Posture & Ergonomics

Assessment and correction of postural imbalances with workplace ergonomic advice for long-term spinal health.
